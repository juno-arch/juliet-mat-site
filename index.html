The method

What is Muscle Activation?

MAT® is a hands-on process developed over 30 years ago by biomechanics specialist Greg Roskopf, used today by professional athletes and everyday bodies alike. It looks at pain and tightness through a different lens: when a muscle loses clear communication with the nervous system, it quietly goes offline, and neighboring muscles tighten up to protect the joint it was supposed to steady.

Stretching, massage, and adjustments work on the tight, protective side of that equation. MAT works on the cause: it finds exactly which muscles have gone quiet and wakes them back up with precise palpation and low-force isometrics.

Tight muscles are working overtime to cover for the ones that fell asleep. The core idea behind MAT®

The process

What a session looks like

  1. AssessA comparative range-of-motion exam, side to side, shows where your body is limiting itself.
  2. TestPosition-specific muscle tests check whether each muscle answers when asked. A quiet response is information, not a verdict.
  3. ActivatePrecise fingertip pressure at the muscle’s attachment points and low-force isometrics restore the connection.
  4. Re-testThe same tests, re-run on the spot. The change is measured, not promised.

What to expect: you stay fully clothed; the work is gentle and specific, no cracking, no deep-pressure bracing. Plan on weekly sessions for about the first six weeks, then taper to maintenance as your body holds the work.
